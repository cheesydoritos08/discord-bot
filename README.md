# discord-bot

Created a silly little bot for the community I'm in. Quick disclaimer, I am NOT an expert at coding and a lot of the code is probably inefficient or not best practice so please take my coding with a grain of salt. If you want to learn more about it, keep reading!

The Lookism Bot is a character collection bot that allows you to collect your favorite characters and level them up to become stronger and fight your friends and other users of the bot! The main features are:

‣ Buying items from the shop: Use the ?shop command to view the shop and checkout all the items you can buy. The most important ones are the standard and limited time banner tickets which allow you to roll for new characters but other items such as XP and yen boosters can be bought from the shop as well.

‣ Rolling for characters: Use the ?roll limited or standard banner command to roll for characters from Lookism! If you roll on the standard banner, the highest rarity you can get is Epic. But, if you roll on the limited time banner, there is a small chance of rolling a LEGENDARY character. The legendary character on the limited time banner rotates every week so make sure to get as many copies of the limited time character as you can before it rotates!

‣ Fighting other players: After getting characters, you can add them to your team with the ?addteammember command and begin fighting with them. Use the ?challenge command to challenge a user to a fight. Depending on whether you win or lose, your ELO will go up or down. Check the leaderboard with the ?leaderboard command to see global rankings! After reaching a certain ELO, your ranking will increase and you will gain a passive yen bonus! The ranks are as follows:

➼ None: Have an ELO of below 1200, no boost
➼ Bronze: Have an ELO of at least 1200 ELO, passive boost of 5%
➼ Silver: Have an ELO of at least 1500 ELO, passive boost of 10%
➼ Gold: Have an ELO of at least 1800 ELO, passive boost of 15%
➼ Diamond: Have an ELO of at least 2100 ELO, passive boost of 20%
➼ Amethyst: Have an ELO of at least 2400 ELO, passive boost of 25%
